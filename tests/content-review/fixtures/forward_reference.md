# Northbridge Pilot Operating Standard

The public opening date approved by the sponsor is 15 September 2026.

Silence beyond forty-eight working hours is prohibited whenever a participant seeks an answer from the service.

Participant records must be destroyed no later than six months after the participant leaves the pilot.

Before any personal information is transferred to an external delivery organisation, the participant must provide written consent.

Monthly dashboards and the final evaluation may be shared with the sponsor and the two neighbourhood centres.
