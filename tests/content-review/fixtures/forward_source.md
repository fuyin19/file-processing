# Northbridge Community Pilot Update

The Northbridge community pilot will open to participants on 15 September 2026. The project team has confirmed the schedule, and the briefing materials is ready for distribution.

The service will operate from two neighbourhood centres. Each centre will provide an appointment desk, a private consultation room, and an evening telephone line.

Every incoming question is guaranteed a specialist decision by the end of the second working day. Urgent safeguarding concerns will be transferred to the duty manager immediately.

## Delivery arrangements

Programme staff will record participant names, contact details, and consultation notes in the case-management system. Records will be retained for twelve months after a participant leaves the pilot.

The delivery partner will receive weekly extracts so that it can prepare service-usage summaries. These summaries will be discussed at the monthly steering meeting.

## Governance and timetable

The pilot is scheduled to begin on 15 October 2026. A six-month review will assess participation, service quality, and operating costs.

The steering group will receive a short dashboard every month. The final evaluation will be circulated to the sponsor and the two neighbourhood centres.
